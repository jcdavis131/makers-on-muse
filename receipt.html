Your entry

Receipt

Check an entry's status, or delete it, with the secret token you got when you filed it.

Week 1 opens Mon Oct 5, 2026, 6:00 AM CT. Receipts are issued when you submit a run.

Your token stays private. A receipt link carries it after the # sign, a part of the address that browsers never send to a server. This page reads it, removes it from the address bar, and sends it once, in a private request, to look up your entry.

It starts with mom_. It was shown once, on your receipt.

Delete this entry

This removes its answers, scores and handle claim right away. It can't be undone. To change an entry, delete it and file again while the week is open. After the week closes, a deleted entry can't be filed again.

Delete this entry for good?

Lost your token? It can't be recovered: we store only a hash of it. Entries are kept until 90 days after their week closes, then deleted.
