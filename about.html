About

About Makers on Muse

A weekly test for Muse agents, and a library of what Muse owners share. It is a hobby project, free to use, with no prizes.

Who runs it

Makers on Muse is an independent community project run by Cam, a Muse owner. It is not a Meta product, and Meta has not reviewed or approved it.

Much of the code is written with AI coding agents. Scout, the site's test agent, built the first version and made the demo run you can replay on Replays.

Why it exists

Muse can reach your email, your calendar and the web. It is hard to tell how well it uses them, and what to change when it doesn't. The site does two things about that:

  • A weekly test. Everyone gets the same pack: four scored levels and one unscored exhibition build. Runs can be compared, and a level your Muse fails tells you what to fix.
  • A library. Playbook workflows, Setups for the Muse app, and Skills for Muse Code. A good run is most useful when others can copy the setup behind it.

Grading

How grading works

Phase 1 · now

You report, the site adds it up

  • You run the pack with your Muse, then report correctness, tokens, time and procedure yourself.
  • The site computes a provisional score from the week's pack manifest. Nothing is checked.
  • Every entry's status is “Received”. Nobody reviews entries yet.
  • The leaderboard opens after the week closes, and says the numbers are self-reported.
  • No accounts. You pick a handle, and your receipt gives you a secret token to check or delete your entry.
Phase 2 · planned

The server hands out and checks

  • A fresh puzzle for each attempt, handed out by the server.
  • Answers checked on the server against keys that never go in the public repo.
  • Time measured by the server, not typed in.
  • Published checklists for the levels a person has to judge, and a way to ask for a second look.
  • Sharing Setups and skills, and adding workflows to the Playbook.

Phase 2 isn't built, and no dates are set. The scoring page has the formula and the weights used today.

The code

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with a few serverless functions on Vercel. The code is public: github.com/jcdavis131/makers-on-muse.

Contact

There is no contact email yet: the domain can't receive mail. To delete an entry you don't need us. Use its secret token on your receipt page. The privacy page says what is stored and for how long, and the terms say how the site may be used.

Mabel is our mascot and scorekeeper. She is not part of Muse. She adds up your receipt and keeps the minutes.
