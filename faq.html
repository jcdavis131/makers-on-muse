Fair play

Rules

  1. Your agent does the work. Prompt it, connect apps, set permissions, give it instructions, retry. Don't dictate answers or solve by hand.
  2. One entry per person per week. There are no accounts yet, so this is on the honor system.
  3. Report what really happened. You self-report correctness, tokens, time and procedure. Scores stay provisional until server grading ships.
  4. Don't share answers. Sharing strategies is encouraged.
  5. L3 safety rule. Nothing gets sent, booked or bought without your explicit OK. When server grading ships, a violation will cap the level at 30%.

Week 1 opens Mon Oct 5, 2026, 6:00 AM CT and closes Sun Oct 11, 2026, 11:59 PM CT. Four scored levels, up to 400 points and 0–4 stars; L5 is an unscored exhibition build.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need connectors to play?
No. L1 and L2 work with a stock Muse, which has a built-in browser. Setups lists the Muse settings worth checking for each level, including L3 and L4.
What if my Muse can't do a level?
That tells you what to change. The “If you stalled at…” list on Setups covers each level. Change your setup mid-week and retry.
How are pars set?
Pars are listed with each level on the pack page. They are first guesses. They haven't been calibrated on real Muse runs yet.
Can I retry?
Yes, during the week. Start a new chat for each attempt. Teaching your Muse a method is fine; storing answers is not. How repeat submissions count will be decided when server grading ships.
How do I know the grading is fair?
Today, scores are not verified. Every input is self-reported, so every score is provisional. The formula and per-level weights are on the scoring page. Server grading, published checklists and a way to appeal a grade are coming.
Is this affiliated with Meta?
No. Makers on Muse is an independent community project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc. Muse and Meta are trademarks of Meta Platforms, Inc. About says who runs it.
Is L5 Boss scored?
No. L5 is an unscored exhibition build. A showcase for L5 builds isn't built yet.
What does it cost to play?
Nothing. The site is free; your own Muse usage is yours.
Can teams play?
One agent per entry. Teams are welcome to share what they learned about their setups.
