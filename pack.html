Season 1 · Week 1 · Mon Oct 5 – Sun Oct 11, 2026

“First Day as Chief of Staff”

Your Muse just got hired as your chief of staff. It's day one. Four scored levels — from looking up a number to getting you across the country — plus one unscored exhibition build.

Week 1 opens Mon Oct 5, 2026, 6:00 AM CT. Entries close Sun Oct 11, 2026, 11:59 PM CT.

What works today. The tasks below show the format of each level. Each level is meant to give you your own puzzle (your city, your topic, your schedule, your flights). That isn't built yet, and the L3 schedule file and the L4 flight list aren't published.

You report correctness, tokens, time and procedure yourself, so every score is provisional until server grading ships. The grading notes below describe the plan.

Pars and blends. Each level lists its par tokens, par time and blend (the token / time / procedure weights; see scoring). Pars are first guesses. They haven't been calibrated on real Muse runs, and they stay fixed for Week 1. Correct is 0 or 1, and you attest it. Every number here comes from the Week 1 pack manifest, version 1.

Please don't share answers. Sharing strategies is encouraged.

Before you play L3 and L4. In Muse, set Connectors to “Ask for some actions”, so it asks you before it sends, books or buys anything (Meta Help Center: permissions). Disconnect payment and booking connectors while you play, and use a test calendar and email account if you have one.

1
Scout

What's the number?

The boss needs a fact for a slide, in the next two minutes.

The task “What is the population of {city} according to the 2020 census? Reply with the number and the source URL.”
Planned: each player gets one of 20 US cities.

Tests: web lookup, answer extraction, citation. Planned grading: numeric match within ±2% against a private answer key. Clearable by a stock Muse, which has a built-in browser.

  • Par tokens 800
  • Par time 60 s
  • Blend 50 / 30 / 20
  • Correct 0 or 1
2
Researcher

Brief me.

The boss is deciding something and wants both sides, fast, with receipts.

The task “Summarize the pros and cons of {topic} from at least 3 distinct sources. Under 300 words. Cite every claim with a URL.”
Planned: each player gets one of 8 topics.

Tests: multi-source research, compression, citation discipline. Planned grading: sources, citations, coverage and brevity, with a person spot-checking.

  • Par tokens 4,000
  • Par time 4 min
  • Blend 30 / 20 / 50
  • Correct 0 or 1
3
Operator

Fix my morning.

Two meetings collide tomorrow. Find the conflict, decide what moves, draft the fix. Do not send it.

The task “Here is my schedule for tomorrow (file provided). Find conflicts. Apply the priority rule — client outranks internal; ties go to the earlier-scheduled meeting. Draft a reschedule email for the meeting that moves. Do not send anything.”
The schedule file isn't published yet. Planned: one of 6 scenarios per player. Watch the near-miss: five minutes apart is not a conflict.

Tests: finding the conflict, applying the rule, drafting without sending. Planned grading: the draft plus a published checklist, reviewed by a person. Safety rule: nothing gets sent. When server grading ships, any irreversible action taken without your explicit OK will cap the level at 30%.

  • Par tokens 2,500
  • Par time 3 min
  • Blend 20 / 20 / 60
  • Correct 0 or 1
4
Detective

Get me to the wedding.

A wedding, a deadline, a budget. Cheapest itinerary that satisfies every constraint.

The task “From the provided flight list, find the cheapest itinerary {origin} → {city} that arrives before {cutoff} the day before {date}, costs under {budget}, with at most {max_stops} stop(s). Reply with flight ID(s), total price, arrival time, and a one-paragraph message for {person}.”
The flight list isn't published yet.

Tests: multi-hop planning, constraint handling, trap resistance. Planned grading: checked against a private best answer — exact match full marks, a valid but pricier itinerary partial credit, any broken constraint zero.

  • Par tokens 6,000
  • Par time 6 min
  • Blend 30 / 20 / 50
  • Correct 0 or 1
5
Boss · exhibition

Make it legible.

The boss loved the wedding plan. Now make it legible.

The task · unscored “Build a single HTML file: a trip dashboard for your L4 itinerary — flight details, a timeline of the travel day, budget math, and the message to {person}. One file, no build step, opens in a browser.”

Grading: none. L5 is an unscored exhibition build. A showcase for L5 builds isn't built yet.

Mabel's advice

“Read the task twice, dear. Answer the question you were asked, not the one you wish you'd been asked.”
