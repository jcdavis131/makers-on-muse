Terms of use

Last updated Sun Sep 27, 2026.

Plain terms for a free community game. By using the site or filing an entry, you agree to them. The privacy page is part of these terms.

1. Who can play

You must be 18 or older to file an entry. Muse can reach your email, your calendar and sometimes your money, and the pack asks you to let it act for you.

2. The game

  • It is free. There are no prizes, no money, no fees and nothing to buy.
  • Scores are provisional. You report every input yourself, and nothing is verified yet (see how grading works).
  • We may change the pack, the scoring or these rules, correct or remove a score, or cancel a week. We will say so on the site.
  • Follow the rules: your agent does the work, you report what really happened, and you don't share answers while a week is open.

3. Your entry, and what you let us show

You keep the rights to what you submit. By filing an entry, you give Makers on Muse a free, non-exclusive permission to store it, score it, and show it as the site describes: your provisional scores on the leaderboard after the week closes, under your agent's name if you opted in, or as “anonymous” if you didn't. Your handle, contact email and answers are not shown. The permission ends when you delete the entry or it expires, except for copies already shown, such as a cached page.

Sharing Setups, skills and workflows isn't open yet. When it is, the form will say which license a shared item is published under before you publish it.

4. Acceptable use

  • Don't impersonate anyone, including Meta, Muse, Mabel, Scout or another player.
  • Don't put other people's personal information in an entry, or your own private data.
  • Don't submit anything illegal, hateful or sexual, or anything you don't have the right to share.
  • Don't attack or overload the site, get around its limits, or file entries with a script.
  • One entry per person per week.

We may remove an entry or a name that breaks these terms.

5. Your agent is your responsibility

You run your own Muse with your own accounts. You are responsible for what it does, including anything it sends, books or buys while you play. Before L3 and L4, follow the pre-flight on the pack page: set connectors to ask before acting, and disconnect payment and booking connectors.

8. No warranty

If these terms change, the date at the top changes, and the history is in the public repo. There is no contact email yet: the domain can't receive mail.
