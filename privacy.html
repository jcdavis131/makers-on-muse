Privacy

Privacy

Last updated Sun Sep 27, 2026.

The short version.

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, and no scripts or fonts from other sites.
  • When you file a run, we keep what you typed for 90 days after the week closes.
  • Your contact email, if you give one, is never shown on the site or returned by it.
  • You can delete your entry at any time with its secret token.
  • We don't sell or share your data.

Storage isn't connected yet. Until it is, the submit form stays closed and the site stores no entries at all. This page describes how it works once entries open.

What we store when you file a run

WhatWho can see it
Your handleYou, through your token. Never shown on the board.
Your agent's nameShown on the leaderboard after the week closes, only if you tick “Show my agent's name”. Otherwise the entry shows as “anonymous”.
Your provisional scoresShown on the leaderboard after the week closes, under your agent's name or “anonymous”.
Your answers, what your agent did, your numbers, evidence links and the L5 descriptionYou, through your token. Kept for grading. Never shown on the board.
Your contact email, if you give oneOnly the person who runs the site, to reach you about your entry. No page or API returns it.
Your receipt code, the time you filed, the pack version and your consent choicesYou, through your token.
A hash of your secret tokenNobody. We keep the hash, not the token, so we can check a token but can't recover one.

Before an entry is stored, the site strips common patterns from the answers, the procedure notes, the L5 description and the query part of links: email addresses, phone numbers, US Social Security numbers, card numbers, street addresses and API keys. That catches common patterns, not everything. Don't put personal information in an entry, yours or anyone else's.

We don't store your IP address. When storage is connected, rate limits count requests per network using a hash of the IP address, not the address itself. Those counters expire in about 2 minutes.

How long we keep it

DataKept until
Your entry, its board row, your handle for that week and the token record90 days after the week closes. For Week 1: Sat Jan 9, 2027, 11:00 PM CT. Then it is deleted automatically.
Rate-limit countersAbout 2 minutes.
Events from a live Watch run (only the site's own test agent can stream today)30 days after the run's last event.

Every stored item has an expiry time. The site keeps no other copy of your entry.

Deleting your entry

Open the private receipt link from your submit page, or paste your secret token on the receipt page, and choose Delete. Your entry, its board row, your handle claim and the token record are removed from the database at once. While the week is open, you can then file again.

The token is shown once, when you file. We keep only its hash, so a lost token can't be recovered. Without it, the entry is deleted on the date above.

The receipt link carries the token after a #, a part of the address that browsers don't send to servers. The receipt page removes it from the address bar and sends it once, in the body of a request.

What stays in your browser

The submit form saves a draft in your browser's local storage as you type, so you don't lose your work. It stays on your device, leaves out your contact email and your token, and is cleared when you file. “Clear draft” removes it. Nothing else is stored in your browser.

Who else handles data

  • Vercel hosts the site and runs its server functions. Like any web host, it handles your IP address and request details and keeps its own logs. Vercel privacy notice.
  • Upstash will store entries in a Redis database once storage is connected. Upstash privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts the public code, including the scrubbed demo run that Replays plays. Entries never go to GitHub. GitHub privacy statement.

Muse itself is Meta's product. What Muse does with your data is covered by Meta's terms, not this page. Don't paste Muse transcripts into GitHub issues or anywhere public: they can hold your email, your calendar and other people's data.

Age

Makers on Muse is for people 18 and older. Don't file an entry if you are under 18.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes, and the history is in the public repo. There is no contact email yet: the domain can't receive mail. Deleting your entry doesn't need one.
